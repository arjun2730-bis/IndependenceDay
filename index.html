🇮🇳India's Independence Day🇮🇳

15 August is a special day for India. India became independent in 1947.

Why Do We Celebrate?

We celebrate Independence Day to remember the people who helped India become free. It is also a day to celebrate our country.

15 August 1947

On 15 August 1947, India became independent from British rule.
Jawaharlal Nehru became the first Prime Minister of India.
